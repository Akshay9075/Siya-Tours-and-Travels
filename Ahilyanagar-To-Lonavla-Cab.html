The name Lonavala comes from "Len" meaning a resting place carved from stone, and "Avali" meaning sequence. Lon-avali means a place with a line of resting places from stones. Siya Tours & Travels offers the best cab service to Lonavala and nearby areas for an unforgettable experience.

Sight-Seeing Information of Lonavala

At Lonavala, you'll discover numerous beautiful sight-seeing points, lakes, and dams. It takes two to three days for a relaxed stay to visit all attractions. Popular sightseeing places include:

  • Rajmachi Fort
  • Tigers Leap
  • Bhushi Dam
  • Duke's Nose Point
  • Karla Caves
  • Ekveera Temple
  • Lonavala Lake
  • Lions Points
  • Lohagad Fort
  • Bhaja Caves
  • Karnala Bird Sanctuary
  • Bhedse Caves

Around Lonavala, there are several wax museums and adventure parks like Adlabs Imagica and Della Adventure Park. This serene destination is the perfect weekend getaway for spending quality time with friends and family.
