Travel Beaches and the Konkan Coast by Renting a Cab to Ganpatipule: The beaches of Ganpatipule are clean and unpolluted, known for white sand and clean seawater. During winter, you will find water sports activities at Ganpatipule. This beach is an ideal place to spend time with family.
