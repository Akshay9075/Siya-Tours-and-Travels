Sight-Seeing Information of Chh. Sambhajinagar

  • The World Heritage site of the Magnificent Ajanta and Ellora Caves
  • Bibi Ka Maqbara - Remarkable monument with architectural beauty

  • Aurangabad Caves - Buddhist caves dating back to 6th century
  • Grishneshwar Jyotirlinga - One of the five Jyotirlinga of Maharashtra
