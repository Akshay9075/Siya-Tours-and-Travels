Sightseeing Information of Nagpur

Nagpur holds historical and geographical prominence with beautiful sightseeing localities, National Tiger Sanctuaries, Temples, and Lustrous Lakes. Must visit places near Nagpur are:

  • Deeksha bhoomi: A sacred monument of Buddhism where B.R. Ambedkar converted to Buddhism.
  • Ramtek Fort Temple: A temple inside a fort on a hilltop with rich mythological history.
  • Ambazari Lake: The largest of the 11 lakes in Nagpur, surrounded by mango trees.
  • Amba Khori: Mesmerising waterfalls situated close to the banks of River Pench.
  • Akshardham Temple: Swaminarayan Temple located on the Ring Road with modern facilities.
  • Maharaja Bagh and Zoo: A botanical garden and zoo housing rare species of flora and fauna.
  • Shri Ganesh Temple Tekri: The temple boasts rich architecture with an idol of Ganesh.
  • Sai Baba Temple: A beautiful temple with an idol similar to the one placed in Shirdi.
  • Dragon Palace Buddhist Temple: Also known as the Lotus Temple, established in 1999 with a huge sandalwood statue of Buddha.
  • Narrow Gauge Rail Museum: A museum with models of locomotives, trains, and an amusement park for kids.
