Distance and Travel Time from Ahilyanagar to Nashik

The distance by road from Ahilyanagar to Nashik is nearly 210 km and can be covered by cab in around 4.5 hours comfortably. The weather in Nashik is always pleasant. In summers, it usually goes up to 40°C. There will be moderate rains during monsoon (June to September). Winter is pleasant around 30°C but drops to 15°C at night. October to March is the best time to visit.
